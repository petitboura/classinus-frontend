## 1. Dev.to : https://dev.to/new

Étiquettes : `ai`, `africa`, `webdev`, `buildinpublic`
Image de couverture : `classinus-mobile-money.png`

**Titre**
```
Card declined? I built an AI platform you can pay for with mobile money
```

**Texte**
```
I'm Bourama, solo founder of Classinus (I built it, so this is a disclosure).

The problem was simple: most AI services expect an international card. In Africa, mobile money is how many people actually pay. So a lot of developers can't even subscribe to the tools everyone talks about.

## What Classinus is

Classinus puts the most powerful AI models in one subscription: Claude, GPT, Gemini and more. You pay with Orange Money or Wave, no international card needed.

It is not just a chat window:

- A code sandbox: the AI runs your code in an isolated environment, and asks for your approval before sensitive actions
- Connectors to your apps and services, including your own custom MCP servers
- Skills: set up how the AI works once, reuse it everywhere
- Web and document search, and file creation (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Web, Android and Windows, in French and English

You can try it without creating an account: https://classinus.com

## Why one subscription for several models

Every new model is best at something different. I stopped asking "which model is the best?" and asked "why should I have to choose?"

## What was hardest

Getting the first users, and keeping the interface simple with so many features.

## Feedback I'd love

How the sandbox and connectors feel, what to build first, and anything confusing in the first minute.
```
