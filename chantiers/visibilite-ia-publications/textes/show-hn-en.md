## Commentaire d'ouverture

Hi HN, I'm Bourama, a solo founder based in Tunis.

Classinus is a platform that gives access to the top AI models, built with Africa in mind, together with the tools to actually get work done: a sandbox for running code, connectors to apps and services (the ones we provide plus your own custom ones), customizable skills, web and document search, and file creation (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint). It runs on the web, Android and Windows, in French and English.

You can try it without creating an account.

I care most about three things: quality, speed, and what this can become for people who are usually left out of the best AI tools.
