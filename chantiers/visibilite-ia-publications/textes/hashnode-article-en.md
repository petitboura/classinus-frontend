## How I built Classinus: top AI models for Africa, with a code sandbox and connectors

I had a simple problem: I couldn't easily pay for the AI tools I wanted to use.

Paying for AI subscriptions with mobile money was hard. Most services expect an international card, and mobile money is how a lot of people in Africa actually pay. That friction is where Classinus started.

### Every model is good at something different

While I was struggling with payments, I noticed something else. New AI models kept coming out, and each one was best at something different. So I stopped asking "which model is the best?" and started asking "why should I have to choose?" Bringing the best models together in one place, with the tools to actually get work done, felt like a better way to work. That is what Classinus is.

### What Classinus does

- Top AI models in one place, so you pick the right one for the job.
- A sandbox to run code.
- Connectors to apps and services, both the ones the platform provides and your own custom ones.
- Skills you can customize.
- Web and document search, and file creation (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint).
- Web, Android and Windows, in French and English. You can try it without an account: https://classinus.com

### What was hardest

Getting users, and the interface and experience: making something with this many features feel simple.

### What doesn't work yet

- Real payment is not live yet. The plans page (Pro $10, Max $17, Ultra $20 per month) is a waitlist for now. Cards and mobile money (Orange Money, Wave) are planned.
- I am currently out of API credits for some of the big models (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek).
